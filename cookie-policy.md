# Cookie Policy - CleverPocket Dev

*Last updated: May 4, 2026*

This Cookie Policy explains how CleverPocket Dev uses cookies and similar technologies, such as local storage, on our website. We respect your privacy and aim to be fully transparent about the data stored on your device.

## 1. What are cookies and local storage?
Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help a website provide a better user experience. Local storage is a standard web technology that allows a website to store information locally on your browser.

## 2. How we use them
Our website is built with privacy in mind. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies, third-party advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that monitor your browsing behavior across other sites.

The only storage technologies we currently use are strictly necessary for the site's core functionality and your user experience:
* **Theme Preference (Local Storage):** We use your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) to remember your display preference (Light Mode or Dark Mode) across sessions. This data is not sent to any server and remains solely on your device.
* **Session and Security Cookies:** If you interact with our secure contact forms or beta testing application systems, minimal session cookies may be generated by the secure form processor to prevent spam and maintain a secure session.

## 3. Third-party cookies
We currently do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools on our website. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and request your explicit consent as required by law before placing any non-essential cookies on your device.

## 4. Managing your preferences
You can choose to disable or clear your browser's local storage and cookies at any time through your web browser's settings. Please note that clearing your local storage will simply reset your Light/Dark mode preference to the default setting.
