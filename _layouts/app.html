Become a Beta Tester

Google requires a group of at least 12 testers to be active for 14 consecutive days before an app can be released.
Note: The 14-day countdown starts only once the full group is active. If you join early, you may need to keep the app installed for a longer period until the goal is reached.

Tester Reward:

As a thank you, testers who complete the full testing phase will receive lifetime free access to the app, provided it is successfully published. Participation is voluntary and can be discontinued at any time; however, leaving the program before the completion of the requirements will result in the loss of reward eligibility.

Note: Is recommend joining the test only if you find the app truly useful. The best feedback comes from users who would naturally use the app in their daily routine.
