Become a Beta Tester

Google requires a group of at least 20 testers to be active for 14 consecutive days before an app can be released. Note: The 14-day countdown starts only once the full group is active. If you join early, you may need to keep the app installed for a longer period until the goal is reached.

Tester Reward:

As a thank you, testers who complete the full testing phase will receive lifetime free access to the app, provided it is successfully approved and published on the Play Store.

Note: Is recommend joining the test only if you find the app truly useful. The best feedback comes from users who would naturally use the app in their daily routine.
