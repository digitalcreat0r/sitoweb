# Privacy Policy - CleverPocket Dev

*Last updated: May 4, 2026*

Welcome to **CleverPocket Dev**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, apply to be a beta tester, or contact us with feedback and new ideas.

*(Note: This policy applies exclusively to interactions on our website. Our individual mobile applications have their own specific privacy policies, which can be accessed within each app and on their respective app store pages).*

## 1. Information We Collect
We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when interacting with our website:
- **Contact Information:** When you reach out via our contact forms to share ideas, report bugs, or ask for support, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
- **Tester Applications:** If you apply to become a beta tester for our current or future applications, we may collect your name, email address, Google Play associated email (for testing access), device type, and any other information necessary to manage the testing program.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect exclusively for the following purposes:
- To respond to your inquiries, questions, or feedback.
- To manage and coordinate our beta testing programs (e.g., adding you to Google Play testing tracks, sending you instructions, and gathering bug reports).
- To evaluate your suggestions for new features or applications.
- To send you important updates regarding the apps you are testing or supporting.

*We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.*

## 3. Cookies and Tracking
Our website uses minimal cookies strictly necessary for its core functionality (such as submitting a secure contact form or managing user sessions). We do not use intrusive tracking cookies or sell your browsing data. If we implement third-party analytics tools in the future to understand website traffic, we will update this policy and request your consent where required by law.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services
We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers only to the extent necessary to run our operations (for example, email hosting providers or secure form processors). These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

## 5. Data Retention
We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary:
- **General Inquiries:** Until your question or feedback has been adequately addressed.
- **Beta Testers:** For the duration of the testing period and a reasonable time thereafter to manage lifetime rewards or future testing opportunities, unless you request to be removed from our tester list.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Depending on your location, you have specific rights regarding your personal data. You have the right to:
- Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
- Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Request the deletion of your personal data ("Right to be forgotten").
- Withdraw your consent at any time if you no longer wish to be a beta tester or receive communications from us.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
